Common questions

I bought full access but the app still shows the paywall.
Make sure you are signed in with the same account you bought with, then pull to refresh or reopen the app. On iPhone, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings. Still locked? Email us and we will fix it.

My school enrolled me. How do I get access?
Create an account with the email your school has on file, or sign in if you already have one. Access unlocks automatically. If it does not, your school may have a different email for you: ask them, or email us.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the email link. The link is one-time and expires, so use the newest email.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings, then Delete account. This permanently erases your data and cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email us from your account email and we will delete it within 30 days.

How do refunds work?
Within 7 days of purchase, full refund, no questions asked: email refund@cdlfastpass.com. Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play follow those stores' refund processes. Details in our terms.

Is CDL FastPass affiliated with the DMV?
No. CDL FastPass is an independent study tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by any Department of Motor Vehicles or government agency.

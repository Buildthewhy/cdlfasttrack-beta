Privacy Policy

CDL FastPass · Effective November 23, 2026 · Operated by Off Menu Club LLC, Las Vegas, Nevada · support@cdlfastpass.com

The short version

We collect the minimum needed to run a study app: your email address, your sign-in credentials in secure hashed form, your study progress, your purchase status, and an optional call sign you choose. We do not collect your location or contacts. We do not use tracking or advertising SDKs. We do not show ads. We never sell your data. We do not send your data to any external AI provider. You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address. Used to create and secure your account, and to send essential account messages such as password resets. If your truck driving school enrolled you, the school provided this email so your access unlocks automatically.
  • Authentication data. Your password is stored only in hashed form. We never see or store it in plain text.
  • Study progress events. Which questions you answered, quiz and exam results, review scheduling data, streaks, XP, in-game cash and rig upgrades, and readiness scores. This exists so your progress syncs across devices and survives reinstalls.
  • Entitlement state. Whether you have purchased full access or had it granted by your school, so the app knows what to unlock.
  • Call sign (optional). A display name you choose. It appears to other users on leaderboards and challenges. Pick something that does not identify you if you prefer to stay anonymous; you can change or remove it at any time in Settings.

That is the complete list. We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or browsing history.

2. Things we do not do

  • No location tracking of any kind
  • No access to your contacts
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs
  • No third-party tracking SDKs and no cross-app tracking
  • No sale or rental of personal data, ever
  • No sharing of your data with external AI providers
  • No collection of data from children (see section 9)

3. How we use your information

  • Provide the service: sync progress, restore your account, unlock paid features you have purchased or been granted
  • Secure the service: authenticate you and protect your account
  • Essential communication: account and security messages only. We do not send marketing email unless you separately opt in.

4. Payment processing

Full access is a one-time purchase. On iOS it is bought through Apple's App Store, on Android through Google Play, and on the web through Stripe. Your payment details (card numbers, billing address) go to Apple, Google, or Stripe, not to us. We never see them. We use RevenueCat to validate App Store and Google Play purchases; RevenueCat receives a purchase receipt and your app user identifier for that purpose.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase: database and authentication hosting (email, hashed authentication data, progress events, entitlement state)
  • Apple / Google: payment processing, per their own privacy policies
  • Stripe: web payment processing, per its own privacy policy
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation for App Store and Google Play

6. Data retention

  • Your data is retained while your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, all your personal data is erased (see section 7).
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google, Stripe, or RevenueCat are retained per their own policies and legal requirements; we cannot delete those companies' own transaction records.
  • Server backups that may contain deleted data are rotated out within 30 days.

7. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Settings > Delete account. Deletion permanently erases your account, email, call sign, progress events, and entitlement records from our systems. It cannot be undone; progress is not recoverable after deletion. Purchase records stay with the store you bought through, and the stores' own refund rules continue to apply.

If you cannot access the app, email support@cdlfastpass.com from your account email and we will delete your account within 30 days.

8. Offline use and device storage

Study content and your recent progress are stored on your device so the app works offline. This data syncs to our servers when you reconnect. Authentication tokens are stored using your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, encrypted storage on Android).

9. Children

The app is intended for people preparing for a commercial driver license, who must be adults under United States law. The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Database access is restricted with row-level security so each user can only reach their own records.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. The in-app deletion flow covers erasure; for anything else, contact support@cdlfastpass.com. We do not discriminate against users who exercise privacy rights. Nevada residents: we do not sell covered information as defined by Nevada law, and there is nothing to opt out of.

12. Not affiliated with any DMV

CDL FastPass is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any Department of Motor Vehicles or government agency.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and notify you in the app before the changes take effect.
